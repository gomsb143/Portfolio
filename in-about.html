How I Work

Six things I actually believe

01

Structure enables speed

Governance isn't bureaucracy. It's what gives teams permission to move fast without second-guessing every decision.

02

People carry projects

A clean plan with wrong energy delivers nothing. Team dynamics matter as much as Gantt charts.

03

Clarity is the output

Everyone leaves knowing exactly what happens next. If the next step is unclear, the programme is already drifting.

04

Build it to understand it

I manage AI programmes by day and build tools at night. You can't lead what you don't understand from the inside.

05

Ownership first

I don't escalate problems I can solve. Accountability is a starting point, not something I list as a strength.

06

No single point of failure

Every project needs shared context, documented decisions, and backup ownership. Learned the hard way — changed how I lead.
